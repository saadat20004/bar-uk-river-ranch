Your hosts

A family ranch since 1969.

Erik's Swedish parents, Ulf and Petsy Kahn, bought the ranch in 1969 and raised horses and cattle, at one point 200 head. They rode the trails, fished, swam and camped here. Michelle and Erik now share a few private riverside sites, made to be everything crowded campgrounds are not.

Good to know

  • Sites sit on a bluff above the river, with a 1 to 10 ft drop depending on the season. Keep children and dogs away from the edge.
  • No electric or water hookups. Vehicles must be under 25 ft.
  • Life jackets are provided. Bring your own if your child is under 30 lbs.
  • Fireworks can be loud around July 4th. Plan for pets and little ones.
  • Some sites have uneven ground. Tents can be set higher up on the bank.
